# ⌚ TimeLux Watches — Digital Marketing Project

**TimeLux Watches** is a semester-long Digital Marketing project focused on building a complete **organic digital presence for a luxury watch brand**.

The project covers brand development, website creation, social media marketing, organic content strategy, SEO, content planning, and performance analysis across multiple digital platforms.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

TimeLux Watches was developed as a luxury watch brand targeting **men and women aged 18–40**, including watch lovers, fashion enthusiasts, students, and working professionals.

The brand focuses on providing:

* Premium-quality watches
* Luxury-inspired designs
* Affordable pricing
* Cash on Delivery (COD) across Pakistan
* A stylish and regularly refreshed collection

The main objective was to create a professional online presence using **100% organic digital marketing**, without paid advertisements or influencer spending.

---

* Build a strong and consistent luxury watch brand identity
* Develop a professional and mobile-responsive website
